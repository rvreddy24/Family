# FamilyHubs

Web platform for families living away from aging parents in India, operations coordinators and field care partners. The product supports care requests, parent details, visit evidence, health records and care updates. Three roles: CLIENT, ADMIN and PARTNER.

## Product truth
Operational records are stored in SQLite. Supabase supplies email-confirmed authentication. Care alerts notify operations but do not automatically dispatch emergency services. Payments/payouts are unavailable until a provider is chosen and integrated. WhatsApp supports manual click-to-chat and an optional consent-based Meta Cloud API queue. External services must be configured and accepted before launch.

## Principles
Respect parents' dignity, make next steps clear, show only real records and truthful delivery states, and keep personal care data isolated by account. Never substitute fabricated people, location, medical records or balances for missing data. The experience is silent.

## Brand
Use the supplied FamilyHubs elder-care logo and existing transparent emblem derived from that logo. Warm paper, forest navigation and terracotta actions. Typography: Libre Baskerville display and DM Sans body. Keep desktop operations scanable and field/mobile actions easy to reach. Details live in DESIGN.md.

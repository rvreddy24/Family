# FamilyHubs ? Care, close to home

## Direction
Warm, calm care platform, selected by the user. Family, operations and field partners use one consistent workspace. Operate mode: clear next steps, honest records, restrained expression. Daytime and mobile field use call for a light canvas with strong reading contrast.

## Typography
- Display: Libre Baskerville, Georgia, serif. Regular weight, restrained editorial headings.
- Body: DM Sans, sans-serif. 14px base; 12px secondary copy; readable form labels.

## Colors
Forest navigation #254C3C, warm paper #F7F5EF, ink #243A32, muted #66736B, terracotta actions #A94C2B, sage panels #E8EDDF, dividers #E2E6DD. Status must include a text label.

## Layout
248px desktop navigation rail, 76px top bar, generous 42px content margins. Overview has one sage care summary and a compact count ledger. Tables become stacked records on mobile. At 760px, navigation becomes a drawer and forms fill available width. Panels use a single border and 12px radius. No invented people, medical readings, balances or success claims.

## Behavior
Visible loading and recoverable errors, real empty states, server sessions, private proof images, explicit confirmation for removal. Payment collection and payout remain disabled until provider integration. No generated camera/location fallback. Reduced motion supported.
